Crucial Agricultural Economic Advisory: Transportation & Net Profit

A higher reported mandi price does not automatically make that mandi more profitable! Farmers must account for vehicular freight costs (typically ₹18–₹35/km for light commercial vehicles like Tata Ace / Mahindra Bolero Maxx), mandi market cess (1% in Tamil Nadu), handling and unloading charges, weighment fees, and moisture weight loss during transit. Always calculate your net realization before dispatching produce over long distances.
